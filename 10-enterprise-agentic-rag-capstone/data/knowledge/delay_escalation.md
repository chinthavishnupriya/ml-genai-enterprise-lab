# Synthetic Delay and Escalation Policy

This fictional policy is for project testing only. Use the current approved company policy before operational action.

## Delay escalation

When a shipment is delayed, read the latest event, the reason supplied in the event, and the estimated delivery field. Label any revised delivery date as unconfirmed unless the record explicitly confirms it. For a delay beyond the service window, raise an operations case with the shipment ID, origin, destination, last event time, and documented reason. Do not promise a delivery time without an approved carrier update.

## Severity levels

A delay with a known next scan can remain in standard monitoring. A missed connection, repeated absence of scans, customs hold, or unresolved address exception should be reviewed by the operations queue. A possible safety or temperature excursion should be escalated promptly under the relevant handling policy. Severity is determined by the current approved procedures, not by this demonstration's score.

## Customer communication

Communications should state confirmed facts, the last update time, and the current uncertainty. Do not include private contact details in an audit trail or invent an explanation absent from the source record.
