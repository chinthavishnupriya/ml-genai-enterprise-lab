# Synthetic Cross-Border Logistics Policy

This fictional policy is for retrieval tests, not legal or customs advice.

## Customs clearance

International shipments may require a commercial invoice, an itemized packing list, a shipment or transport document, and any permits or origin documents required for the goods and destination. The exact documents depend on the route, commodity, and destination rules. The local dataset does not determine legal requirements; confirm them with the approved compliance team or customs broker.

## Escalation and holds

When a cross-border shipment is on customs hold, inspect the recorded hold reason and check whether a document review is pending. Route the shipment ID and known missing-document details to the compliance queue. Do not invent an expected release date. Update the recipient only after the responsible team confirms a status change.

## Restricted goods and review

Restricted or regulated goods require review against the applicable route and product rules before dispatch. This fictional corpus is not a current restricted-goods list and must not be used to authorize transport.
