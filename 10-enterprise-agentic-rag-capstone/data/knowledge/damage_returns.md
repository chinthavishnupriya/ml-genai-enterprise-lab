# Synthetic Damage and Returns Guide

This guide is fictional educational material. The company's current approved returns procedure remains authoritative.

## Damaged goods

For a damaged-goods report, record the shipment ID, date observed, packaging condition, and concise factual description. Preserve available package and item photographs and supporting delivery documentation according to approved evidence-retention procedures. Do not speculate about fault or accuse a party based only on a short description. Route the report to a human operations reviewer.

## Returns

A return should be linked to a valid order or shipment reference and routed under the applicable return authorization process. The agent can summarize the policy and identify listed missing documents, but it cannot authorize a refund, determine liability, or override a return policy.

## Evidence handling

Store only the minimum necessary evidence in approved systems. Avoid copying personal details, payment data, or full customer messages into diagnostic logs. Restrict access to claim or damage documents according to internal policy.
