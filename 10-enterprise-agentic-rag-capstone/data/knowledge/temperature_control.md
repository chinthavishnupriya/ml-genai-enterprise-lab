# Synthetic Temperature-Controlled Shipment Policy

This fictional policy is for model demonstration only and must not replace an approved cold-chain procedure.

## Temperature control

Temperature-controlled shipments should remain within the range stated by the shipment's validated product instructions and approved handling plan. Preserve the relevant logger readings and timestamps. A generic knowledge article cannot infer the permitted temperature range for every product.

## Excursion escalation

If a logger reports a possible temperature excursion, route the shipment to the designated quality or operations reviewer promptly. Include the shipment ID, the observed range, timestamps, and logger reference where available. Do not release or dispose of goods based on this demo; a qualified reviewer must assess the evidence and applicable procedure.

## Chain of custody

Keep hand-off times, responsible facilities, seal references where applicable, and logger records in the approved system. Do not fabricate missing measurements or treat an absent reading as proof that temperature remained compliant.
