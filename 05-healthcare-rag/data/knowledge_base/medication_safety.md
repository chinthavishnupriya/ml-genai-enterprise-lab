# Demo patient education guide: medicine safety

This fictional document supports software testing and is not a medication instruction sheet or a substitute for official product labeling or professional advice.

## Questions about medicines

Before taking a medicine that is new to you, read the current official label and ask a licensed clinician or pharmacist about its purpose, directions, potential interactions, and what to do if a dose is missed. Do not guess a dose from a search result or a chatbot response.

Keep a current list of prescription medicines, over-the-counter products, vitamins, supplements, and known allergies to discuss with your healthcare professional. Use medicine only as prescribed or directed on the official label. Do not start, stop, share, or change a prescribed medicine based on an answer generated by this demonstration.

Store medicines according to their official labels and keep them away from children and pets. For a suspected overdose, serious reaction, or immediate threat to safety, contact local emergency services or an appropriate poison information service promptly rather than waiting for an online answer.
