# Demo safety note: urgent symptoms

This fictional educational document is designed to test retrieval of safety information. It does not provide triage or determine whether an individual is safe.

## When not to rely on a chatbot

Severe difficulty breathing, new severe chest pain, loss of consciousness, signs of stroke, uncontrolled serious bleeding, or another immediately life-threatening situation requires urgent help. Contact local emergency services immediately; do not delay care to search this knowledge base or wait for an AI response.

A text-retrieval system cannot measure symptoms, examine a person, confirm a diagnosis, or rule out an emergency. If a situation feels dangerous or rapidly worsening, seek urgent professional assessment.

## Routine questions

For non-urgent health questions, contact a qualified healthcare professional who can consider personal history, medications, allergies, examination findings, and other context that a document search cannot access.
