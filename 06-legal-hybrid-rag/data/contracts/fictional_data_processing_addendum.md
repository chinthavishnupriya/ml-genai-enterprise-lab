# Fictional Data Processing Addendum

This entirely fictional data processing addendum is for testing retrieval software only. It is not a statement of actual legal requirements.

## 1.1 Processing Instructions

The processor may process customer personal data only on the documented instructions of the controller and only for the services specified in the fictional agreement. The processor must notify the controller if an instruction appears to conflict with a binding legal requirement, unless law prohibits notice.

## 2.1 Security Safeguards

The processor must maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the fictional service, including access controls, staff confidentiality commitments, vulnerability management, encrypted transmission, and a documented incident-response process. Access to personal data must be limited to personnel who need it to perform the services.

## 3.1 Personal Data Incident Notice

After becoming aware of a confirmed personal data breach affecting the customer data in scope, the processor must notify the controller without undue delay and, under this fictional clause, no later than forty-eight hours after awareness. The notice should describe the known nature of the incident, categories of affected information where reasonably available, mitigation steps, and a contact for follow-up. The parties will coordinate updates as facts become available.

## 4.1 Subprocessors

The controller gives general written authorization for the processor to use the subprocessors listed in the contract schedule. The processor must notify the controller of a planned subprocessor change at least fifteen days in advance and provide a reasonable opportunity to object on documented data-protection grounds. The processor remains responsible for its subprocessors' performance under this fictional addendum.

## 5.1 Return and Deletion

At the end of the services, the processor must, at the controller's choice, return or delete personal data in its possession, except where a binding retention obligation requires preservation. Any retained data remains subject to the confidentiality and security obligations in the addendum.
