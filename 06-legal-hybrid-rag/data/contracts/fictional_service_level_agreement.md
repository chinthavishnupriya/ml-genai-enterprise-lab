# Fictional Service Level Agreement

This fictional SLA is included only for testing search and citations. Percentages and remedies here are sample terms, not a real service commitment.

## 1.1 Measurement Window and Exclusions

Monthly availability is measured for each calendar month using the service monitoring system designated in the order form. Scheduled maintenance announced at least forty-eight hours in advance, customer-caused outages, and events outside the provider's reasonable control are excluded from the availability calculation as specified in this fictional clause.

## 2.1 Monthly Availability Target

The provider targets monthly service availability of at least 99.5 percent, measured during the monthly measurement window and subject to the exclusions in Clause 1.1. This target does not guarantee that every feature will be uninterrupted, and the SLA's stated service-credit process is the remedy specified for a qualifying availability shortfall.

## 3.1 Service Credits

If monthly availability falls below the 99.5 percent target, the customer may request a service credit equal to five percent of the monthly recurring fee for each complete percentage point below the target, capped at twenty-five percent of that month's recurring fee. The customer must submit the request with the affected month and relevant incident references within thirty days after the end of that month. Approved credits apply to a future invoice and are not paid as cash.

## 4.1 Support Response Targets

Support tickets are assigned severity based on impact and scope. For a Severity 1 outage affecting production use for most users, the provider targets an initial response within one hour. Severity 2 issues target an initial response within four business hours, and standard requests target an initial response within one business day. These are response targets, not guaranteed resolution times.

## 5.1 Reporting and Review

The provider will make a monthly service summary available on request, including reported availability, major incidents, and planned maintenance. The parties may review repeated availability failures during a service review meeting and discuss remediation plans.
