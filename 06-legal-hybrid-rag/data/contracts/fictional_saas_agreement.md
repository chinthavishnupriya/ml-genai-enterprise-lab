# Fictional Software-as-a-Service Agreement

This entirely fictional contract is for testing legal retrieval software only. It does not reflect a real party or legal advice.

## 1.1 Services and Scope

The provider will make the hosted software services described in the applicable order form available to the customer during the subscription term. Any implementation tasks or additional services must be described in a signed order form. The customer is responsible for maintaining compatible devices, credentials, and network access.

## 3.1 Subscription Term and Renewal

The initial subscription term begins on the start date in the order form and continues for twelve months unless the order form specifies another period. The term automatically renews for successive twelve-month renewal terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty days before the current term ends.

## 4.1 Fees and Invoicing

The provider invoices subscription fees annually in advance unless the order form sets a different schedule. Each undisputed invoice is due within thirty calendar days after the invoice date. The customer must give written notice of a good-faith invoice dispute within fifteen days of receipt and must pay all undisputed amounts by the due date. Fees are stated exclusive of applicable taxes.

## 4.2 Late Payments

For undisputed amounts that remain overdue, the provider may charge a late fee at the lower of one percent per month or the maximum amount permitted by the governing law stated in the order form. The provider must give written notice and a ten-day opportunity to cure before suspending service solely for non-payment.

## 5.1 Confidentiality

Each party must protect the other party's confidential information using at least reasonable care and may use that information only to perform or receive services under this agreement. Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that becomes public without breach, was already lawfully known, is independently developed, or must be disclosed by law after reasonable notice where permitted.

## 6.1 Limitation of Liability

Except for the excluded claims listed in this clause, each party's aggregate liability arising from the agreement will not exceed fees paid or payable by the customer during the twelve months before the event giving rise to the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential loss to the extent allowed by the governing law. This clause is a fictional sample and does not state a universal legal rule.

## 7.1 Termination for Material Breach

Either party may terminate this agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches the agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty days after receiving written notice that describes the breach. A party may terminate immediately if the other party becomes insolvent and the applicable law permits immediate termination. Accrued payment obligations survive termination.
